Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you use Weather Spectrum (theweatherspectrum.com), a weather radar viewer. Weather Spectrum does not offer accounts, does not ask for your name, email or location, and does not sell personal information.

Hosting

The site and its lightning data service run on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in order to deliver and protect the site. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Analytics (only with your consent)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used, such as pages visited, approximate location (city or region level), device and browser type. Google Analytics is loaded only if you select "Accept All" in the cookie banner. If you decline, it is not loaded. Google Analytics sets cookies (for example _ga). See Google's privacy policy and the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. To change your choice, clear this site's data in your browser and choose again.

Radar, map and lightning data services

When you use the radar, your browser requests map and weather data directly from the services below. Each of them receives your IP address, browser information and the map area being requested, and is governed by its own policies:

  • NOAA / National Weather Service map services (opengeo.ncep.noaa.gov) for radar imagery and national radar mosaics.
  • NOAA NEXRAD Level III data on Amazon Web Services (unidata-nexrad-level3.s3.amazonaws.com), published through the NOAA Open Data Dissemination program.
  • Iowa Environmental Mesonet, Iowa State University (mesonet.agron.iastate.edu), for radar imagery and scan times.
  • OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org) for the background map.
  • Weather Spectrum lightning service on Cloudflare, which serves lightning data processed from NOAA GOES satellite files.

We do not share any personal information with these services beyond what your browser sends when loading data.

Information stored in your browser

  • cookieConsent: remembers whether you accepted or declined analytics.
  • ws.radar.view and ws.radar.opacity: remember your last map position and radar opacity.
  • Google Analytics cookies, only if you accepted analytics.
  • Data stored by OneSignal for push notifications (see below).

The radar's map position is stored only in your browser and is never sent to us. The site does not request your device's location.

Push notifications

The site loads OneSignal's software to support web push notifications. If you have subscribed, OneSignal stores a push token for your browser and related device data in order to deliver notifications. You can unsubscribe at any time in your browser's site settings. See OneSignal's privacy policy.

Advertising

Weather Spectrum does not currently display advertising.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

International visitors

Weather Spectrum is operated in the United States. Information handled by the services above may be processed in the United States or other countries.

Your choices and rights

  • Decline analytics in the cookie banner, or clear site data to choose again.
  • Block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings.
  • Unsubscribe from push notifications in your browser's site settings.
  • Contact us with questions or requests about your information.

Changes

We will update this page and the "Last updated" date when this policy changes.
